About Me

I’m based in Los Angeles, USA, and I recently graduated (Dec 2024) from California State University Fullerton with a Bachelor's in Computer Science. My passion for games drives everything I do in tech.

I specialize in creating engaging, intuitive mechanics that keep players hooked. Developing games is something I truly enjoy, but I’m even more passionate about collaborating with others. I’m a self-proclaimed game jam junkie, having participated in 18 game jams over the past two years, 7 of which I’ve led as the project lead. I thrive in collaborative settings, and I’m always eager to contribute to creative problem-solving, whether leading a project or working as part of a team.

When I’m not developing games or learning more about game development, you’ll often find me playing video games (big surprise), especially in the action, rogue-like, or strategy genres. Outside of gaming, I enjoy watching comedic TV shows, scream singing songs at karaoke spots or in the shower, and taking leisurely strolls around my neighborhood.
