About Me

I’m based in Los Angeles, USA, and I recently graduated (Dec 2024) from California State University Fullerton with a Bachelor's in Computer Science. My passion for games drives everything I do in tech.

I specialize in creating engaging, intuitive mechanics that keep players hooked. Developing games is something I truly enjoy, but I’m even more passionate about collaborating with others. I’m a self-proclaimed game jam junkie, having participated in 18 game jams over the past two years, 7 of which I’ve led as the project lead. When I’m not developing games or learning more about game development, you’ll often find me playing video games (big surprise), especially in the action, rogue-like, or strategy genres.
